Read_Me: Hangman Project
Project Overview
The game is Hangman. You are given a secret word and in order to win you have to guess what letters are in the word. You can only guess one at a time and you have six attempts. Each time you guess incorrectly you loose an attempt and add a body part to the hang post. Every time you guess right it will reveal the letter and you become one step closer to solving the word. There are three levels: easy, medium and, hard. The changes between them are that easy has 3-5 letter words, medium has 6-8 letter words, and hard has 9 or more letter words. The amount of attempts or incorrect guesses does not change between levels, even though the letters in the words are increasing.
